Delivery Policy

Last updated 3 October 2026

This is a digital coaching service. Nothing will be shipped to you.

After payment

After payment, Grant will email you a private CoachNow invitation and simple instructions for sending your swing videos. Please check your spam or junk folder if you do not see the email.

Your first lesson

Send face-on and down-the-line swing videos if possible, plus a description of what the ball is doing and what you want to improve. A smartphone is all you need. Your personal lesson will be delivered through CoachNow within 48 hours after Grant receives your complete videos and information.

Your follow-up review

Your included follow-up review may be used anytime within two weeks of receiving your first lesson. Practise the recommended Control, then send an updated swing video through CoachNow.

Questions or delays

If your invitation or lesson has not arrived within the expected time, email contact@pureimpactgolf.online.
