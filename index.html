Before you start

What is included?

You receive one personal online lesson and one follow-up review through CoachNow. I’ll assess your ball flight, impact and swing, explain what I see, and give you a clear Control and practice priority.

Is this a subscription?

No. It is one US$99 payment for your lesson and follow-up review. There is no automatic renewal or ongoing commitment.

What should I send?

Send face-on and down-the-line swing videos if possible, along with a description of the shot or strike you want to improve. The more clearly you describe what the ball is doing, the more useful my assessment can be.

How does CoachNow work?

CoachNow is the private space where you send your swing videos and I provide your lesson, practice priority and follow-up review.

When do I send my follow-up swing?

After your first lesson, practise your Control and send an updated swing video through CoachNow when you are ready for me to review your progress.

Do I need to be an experienced golfer?

No. I work with the swing you have today and tailor the lesson to your ball flight, impact and the shots you want to improve.
