Questions

Before you start

What is included?

You receive one personal online lesson and one follow-up review through CoachNow. I’ll assess your ball flight, impact and swing, explain what I see, and give you a clear Control and practice priority.

Is this a subscription?

No. It is one US$99 payment for your lesson and follow-up review. There is no automatic renewal or ongoing commitment.

What should I send?

A smartphone is all you need. Send face-on and down-the-line swing videos if possible, along with a description of the shot or strike you want to improve. The more clearly you describe what the ball is doing, the more useful my assessment can be.

How does CoachNow work?

CoachNow is the private space where you send your swing videos and I provide recorded personal feedback, your practice priority and your follow-up review. This is flexible, asynchronous coaching—not a scheduled live call.

How quickly will I receive my lesson?

You’ll receive your personal lesson within 48 hours after I receive your swing videos and information through CoachNow.

When do I send my follow-up swing?

Your follow-up review can be used anytime within two weeks of receiving your first lesson. Practise your Control, then send an updated swing video through CoachNow when you are ready for me to review your progress.

Do I need to be an experienced golfer?

No. I work with the swing you have today and tailor the lesson to your ball flight, impact and the shots you want to improve.
