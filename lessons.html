All Abilities Clinics

Pure Impact Golf will offer All Abilities Clinics at Burleigh Driving Range, building on Grant’s connection with Empower Golf, Australia’s largest inclusive golf provider serving NDIS participants. James Deacon is a PGA of Australia Certified Community Coach and an experienced NDIS support worker.

Clinics are for people with physical and intellectual disabilities who want to try golf, build skills and enjoy the game in a welcoming group. Text Grant to ask about upcoming sessions and discuss what will help you take part.

Meet your coaches

Grant Garrison

Grant has spent more than 40 years as a PGA Golf Professional and coach. He continues to compete and plays off a plus handicap, bringing current playing experience into every lesson. His coaching helps golfers understand their ball flight, identify the changes that matter most and build a simpler, more reliable swing they can trust on the course.

James Deacon

James coaches with Pure Impact Golf, focusing on clear fundamentals, better contact and growing confidence with the game. Whether you are new to golf or looking to sharpen your skills, he aims to make each lesson focused, encouraging and enjoyable.
