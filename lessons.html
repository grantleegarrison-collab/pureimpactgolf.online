Inclusive golf · Empower Golf

All Abilities Clinics

Pure Impact Golf will offer All Abilities Clinics at Burleigh Driving Range, building on Grant’s connection with Empower Golf, Australia’s largest inclusive golf provider serving NDIS participants. James Deacon is an experienced NDIS support worker.

Clinics are for people with physical and intellectual disabilities who want to try golf, build skills and enjoy the game in a welcoming group. Text Grant to ask about upcoming sessions and discuss what will help you take part.

Enquire about All Abilities coaching

Only share information you are comfortable providing. Grant can discuss access and support needs with you before booking.

Grant Garrison

Grant is a PGA Golf Professional and coach, and a 40-year Life Member of the PGA of America. He continues to compete and plays off a plus handicap, bringing current playing experience into every lesson. His coaching helps golfers understand their ball flight, identify the changes that matter most and build a simpler, more reliable swing they can trust on the course. See Grant’s coaching in action on YouTube.

James Deacon

James is a PGA Community Coach with Pure Impact Golf, focusing on clear fundamentals, better contact and growing confidence with the game. Whether you are new to golf or looking to sharpen your skills, he aims to make each lesson focused, encouraging and enjoyable.
