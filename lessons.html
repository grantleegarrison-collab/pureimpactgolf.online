Inclusive golf · Empower Golf

All Abilities Clinics

Pure Impact Golf will offer All Abilities Clinics at Burleigh Driving Range, building on Grant’s connection with Empower Golf, Australia’s largest inclusive golf provider serving NDIS participants.

Clinics are for people with physical and intellectual disabilities who want to try golf, build skills and enjoy the game in a welcoming group. Text Grant to ask about upcoming sessions and discuss what will help you take part.

Text Grant about All Abilities Clinics · Learn about Empower Golf

PGA of America Member

Grant Garrison

Grant brings more than 40 years of coaching experience, TPI Level 2 training and a background in professional competition in Australia. He helps golfers understand their ball flight, focus on the impact changes that matter and practise with purpose.

PGA of Australia · Community Coach

James Deacon

James is a certified Community Coach through the PGA of Australia and an experienced NDIS support worker. He brings a welcoming, practical approach to helping golfers build confidence, develop sound fundamentals and enjoy their progress. His support work informs a patient, considerate approach to golfers with different support needs.
