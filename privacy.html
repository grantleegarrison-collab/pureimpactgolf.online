Privacy Policy

Last updated 3 October 2026

This policy explains how Pure Impact Golf handles the personal information you provide when using the website and personal online coaching service.

Information we collect

  • Your name, email address and contact details.
  • Your swing videos, coaching messages, goals and information about your golf.
  • Purchase and service records. Pure Impact Golf does not receive or store your full card details.

How we use it

We use this information to deliver and support your coaching, communicate with you, maintain business records, improve the service, and meet legal obligations.

Services we use

Stripe processes payments and CoachNow hosts coaching videos and communications. Those providers handle information under their own privacy terms and may process or store information outside Australia.

Website measurement and cookies

With your permission, this website uses Google Analytics to understand visits and interactions such as clicking a lesson button or choosing to play the philosophy video. Button events measure clicks, not completed checkouts or purchases. Google Analytics may use cookies and process technical information such as device, browser and approximate location data. Analytics is not loaded unless you select “Accept analytics.” You may decline or change your choice at any time using “Analytics choices” in the footer. Playing the philosophy video loads an embedded YouTube player; YouTube may receive technical information about your browser and connection and handle it under its own privacy terms. The player loads only after you choose Play.

Access, correction and questions

You may ask about, access or correct personal information held by Pure Impact Golf by emailing contact@pureimpactgolf.online. We will take reasonable steps to protect the information we hold and respond to privacy questions or concerns.
